## ⚙️ Settings & Auto Fill
Open **Settings** to switch on some handy tools:
- **Auto fill** – click a talent and all of its prerequisites are filled in automatically
- **Instant max** – max out a single talent with 1 click
- **Instant zero** – set a single talent to zero with 1 click
- **Speed mode** – skips the tooltips so you can left-click to add and right-click to remove points directly on the nodes
- **Include stats** – adds your stats panel to saved screenshots

## 💻 Best on PC / Laptop
For the best experience, use a **PC or laptop**. It does work on mobile, but the bigger screen makes building much easier. On mobile, pinch to zoom and tap a talent to add points. Turning your phone sideways gives you a wider view.

## ⚠️ Not Available Yet
Some commanders are **not currently available**. They use the newer **Engineering, Smite and Combo** talent branches, which aren't supported yet. We'll be adding them eventually, so stay tuned! Some of the newest commanders also have temporary placeholder portraits until official art is added.
